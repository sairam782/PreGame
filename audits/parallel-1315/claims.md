- **`.gitignore` gap: cassette files.** Only `cassettes/*.local.jsonl` is ignored; `cassettes/demo.jsonl` (54 lines, present on disk, untracked) is **not** covered by any ignore rule and would be picked up by `git add -A`. Worth a deliberate decision (commit intentionally for stage-demo replay, or extend the ignore pattern) rather than letting it land by accident.
